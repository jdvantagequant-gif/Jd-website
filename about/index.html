Who We Are

An engineering firm that
happens to trade

J&D Vantage Quant builds quantitative, fully automated trading systems for Indian markets — NSE, BSE and MCX. We come from engineering, not from the dealing room, and it shows in how we test things before we believe them.

Our mission

To make genuinely institutional-grade algorithmic trading available in India — accessible, transparent and honestly measured. The technology that used to sit behind a large desk's closed door should be something an individual can inspect, question and understand before committing a rupee to it.

Our vision

To be the Indian algo trading firm people trust because the numbers are checkable — where data-driven decisions replace emotional ones, every strategy is tested against real market history with real costs, and regulatory compliance is treated as a constraint rather than a marketing line.

Principles

Four things we do not compromise on

Innovation

Continuous research into Indian market microstructure, execution quality and where an edge actually comes from — rather than recycling a strategy that worked elsewhere.

Transparency

Complete honesty about method, assumption and risk. If a month lost money it stays on the chart. If a cost was incurred it appears in the total.

Client-first

We are paid to build technology and do research, not to move client money. That keeps our incentives pointed at whether the system works, not at how much it trades.

Compliance

We state plainly what we are and what we are not. We do not manage funds, we do not offer PMS, and we do not describe ourselves as registered where we are not.

How we work

The uncomfortable numbers are the useful ones

Any strategy can be made to look good by choosing the window, ignoring the costs or quietly dropping the trades that did not fill. We publish the opposite: the whole window, every cost line, and the worst days alongside the best.

  • Nothing excluded — every calendar month of the window is on the heat grid, red ones included.
  • Costs charged in full — STT, exchange, SEBI, stamp, GST and slippage on every fill.
  • Drawdown shown — the deepest loss and how long it took to recover, not just the return.
  • Trade-level detail — the individual ledger is published, so the aggregate can be checked.
