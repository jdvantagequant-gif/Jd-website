About

An engineering practice, built deliberately narrow

J&D Vantage Quant LLP is a small software development firm in India. We write software for people who need a process automated — most often something involving a market, because that is where our experience is.

01 — The firm

Recently formed, and candid about it

We would rather tell you where we are than dress it up. J&D Vantage Quant LLP is a newly established partnership. We are actively taking on our first client engagements, and the practice has been structured from day one around a single principle: we sell engineering hours and deliverables, and nothing else.

02 — How we work

Four commitments we hold ourselves to

01

The specification is the contract

Behaviour is written down and agreed before code is written. If we disagree about what the software should do, we would rather have that argument on paper at the start than in production later.

02

You own what we build

Source code, documentation and deployment notes are handed over. We do not create dependency by withholding the code, and another engineer can pick up our work.

03

We stay on our side of the line

We build the tool; you operate it. We never take custody of funds, never hold your credentials, and never take a share of what the software earns or loses.

04

We do not sell outcomes

We publish no returns, promise no profit and make no forecast. If a prospective client wants those, we are the wrong firm and we will say so.

04 — Scope

Why we keep the scope this tight

There is an obvious commercial temptation in this sector to drift from building tools into running money — to move from "here is the software you asked for" to "give us the capital and we will handle it". That drift is how firms end up operating as unregistered intermediaries, and how clients end up with no recourse when something breaks.

We have deliberately closed that door. Our engagement letters, our pricing, our marketing and this website are all constructed so that the only thing we can be paid for is engineering work. There is no version of our service in which we hold your money.

It is a smaller business than the alternative. It is also one we can explain, in a single sentence, to a client, a bank or a regulator — and have every document we hold support that sentence.
