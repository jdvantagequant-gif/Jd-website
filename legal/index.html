Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective: September 2026

Before work begins

If you cancel a signed scope of work before development has started, any advance paid is refunded in full.

After work begins

Where an engagement is structured in milestones, milestones already delivered and accepted are non-refundable. Amounts paid against milestones not yet started are refundable. Partially completed work is settled pro rata against time actually spent, evidenced on request.

Maintenance agreements

A maintenance agreement may be cancelled with notice and will run to the end of the period already paid for. We do not issue partial refunds for unused portions of a maintenance period.

Trading losses are not refundable

For the avoidance of doubt: our fees are charged for engineering work delivered. A loss incurred while operating software we built is not a ground for a refund, because we neither control nor participate in the client's trading decisions, accounts or results.

How to raise a request

Email jdvantagequant@gmail.com with the engagement reference and the reason. We aim to respond within seven working days.
