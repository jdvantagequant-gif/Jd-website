Performance Disclosure

Applies to the performance and trade report pages

Backtested, simulated results

The nine-year figures presented on the performance page are backtested, simulated results. They were produced by running the strategy logic over historical market data. They are not a record of live trading over that period, and no capital was actually at risk across the whole of the window shown.

Simulated results have inherent limitations. They benefit from hindsight in the design of the strategy, and they cannot fully reproduce the effect of live order queues, partial fills, rejections, latency or the behaviour of other participants reacting to the same conditions.

How the figures are computed

  • Historical market data is sourced from Dhan across every trading session in the stated window.
  • Slippage of 0.075% per side is applied to both legs of every fill.
  • The full NSE intraday charge stack is recalculated per trade: STT, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover fee, stamp duty, GST and execution costs. The cost breakdown is published in full on the performance page.
  • Positions are floored to whole shares. A signal that cannot fill at least one share at the stated capital is dropped rather than fractionally simulated.
  • Percentage returns are stated on base capital, not on a compounded balance. Position size is a fixed fraction of base capital, so rupee P&L scales with the capital committed while the percentage figures do not.

The trading record

The trade report covers the period from 1 September 2025 to the latest closed session and is presented as a fresh account opened on that date. Returns and drawdown in that window are a share of base capital, not of the larger balance the simulated nine-year curve had reached by then.

Figures may be out of date

The dataset behind these pages is regenerated periodically and carries the date it was generated. Between regenerations the figures shown will lag the current position. Nothing on this site is a live feed.
