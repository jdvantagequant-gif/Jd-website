Compliance

What we are, and what we are not

This page exists so that no client, counterparty, bank or regulator has to guess at the nature of our business. It is written to be read in full and we would rather over-disclose than leave room for inference.

01 — Our position

A software development firm

J&D Vantage Quant LLP is a limited liability partnership carrying on the business of custom software development and process automation. Our clients are businesses and individuals who want a piece of software built. Our income is the professional fee we invoice for building it.

02 — Exclusions

Activities we do not carry on

The following is an exhaustive statement of what we do not do. If any person has been told otherwise about us, that statement was not authorised and we ask to be told about it.

✕ Funds and accounts

  • We do not accept, receive or hold client funds for investment or trading
  • We do not operate a pooled, omnibus or client account of any description
  • We do not have power of attorney or operating rights over any client account
  • We do not route, forward or transmit money between clients and brokers
  • We do not guarantee capital or assure any return to any person

✕ Market activity and advice

  • We do not place, modify or cancel orders on behalf of any client
  • We do not provide investment advice or recommend any security or instrument
  • We do not publish research reports, calls, tips or trade signals to subscribers
  • We do not provide portfolio management or act as a fund in any form
  • We do not broker, deal or arrange deals in securities for any person
03 — Client funds

04 — Fees and receipts

Every rupee we receive is a fee for engineering work

We use two pricing models and no others. Both are fixed in advance, agreed in writing and invoiced. Neither varies with anything that happens in a market.

RECEIPT TYPE 01

One-time development fee

A fixed professional fee for building and delivering software against a written scope of work. Raised as a tax invoice, settled by bank transfer, recognised as services revenue.

RECEIPT TYPE 02

Maintenance and upgrade fee

A periodic or per-change fee for maintaining and upgrading software already delivered. Also invoiced, also fixed in advance, also unrelated to any trading result.

Expressly excluded We do not accept payment structured as a share of profits, a percentage of assets, a brokerage rebate, a commission, a referral fee from a broker, or any other amount whose quantum depends on the client's trading activity or results.
05 — Registration

Why we hold no market registration

Writing software to a customer's specification is a professional services activity. It is not dealing in securities, arranging deals, managing investments, advising on investments or operating a collective investment scheme.

We have deliberately structured every aspect of our offering so that it stays on the software side of that line: the client writes the specification, the client owns the delivered code, the client holds the broker relationship and the credentials, the client runs the system, the client takes the risk, and our fee is fixed and unrelated to the outcome.

Accordingly we hold no registration with the Securities and Exchange Board of India or any other financial regulator, and we make no claim to hold one. We do not describe ourselves as SEBI-registered, SEBI-approved, SEBI-compliant or regulated, and we ask that no client, partner or intermediary describe us that way either.

If a prospective engagement would require us to take custody of funds, exercise discretion over a client's account, or give advice on the merits of an investment, we will decline it. That has been the position from the outset and it is not negotiable.

If you are a bank, auditor or regulator

We will provide, on request and without argument: our LLP incorporation certificate and LLPIN, the partnership agreement, PAN, specimen client contracts, specimen invoices, and a written declaration of the nature of our business signed by a Designated Partner. Write to jdvantagequant@gmail.com and we will respond with documents rather than assurances.

06 — Marketing conduct

What we will never put in front of you

Most of the harm in this industry is done by marketing, not by software. We hold ourselves to the following rules without being required to.

  • No performance figures. We publish no returns, no profit and loss, no equity curves, no win rates and no backtest results anywhere on this website or in any marketing material.
  • No return promises. We never state, project, imply or hint at a return, yield, income or profit that a client might achieve.
  • No signals or tips. We do not operate a signal service, a Telegram tips channel, a subscription calls group or anything resembling one.
  • No investment solicitation. We never invite any person to place money with us, and we would return it if they tried.
  • No borrowed credibility. We do not imply regulatory approval, exchange affiliation or institutional endorsement that we do not have.
Report a misrepresentation If anyone has offered you fund management, guaranteed returns or trading services in our name, they were not authorised to do so. Please tell us at jdvantagequant@gmail.com so we can deal with it.
