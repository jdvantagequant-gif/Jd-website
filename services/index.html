What We Offer

Services built around
evidence, not opinion

Four things we do, all of them quantitative. We build and test trading systems for Indian markets, and we publish what those systems actually produced — including the months they lost money.

01

Custom Algo Strategy Development

Bespoke algorithmic trading systems built to a stated risk budget and a stated objective, for Indian equities, futures, options and commodities.

EquityF&OIndexMCX
  • Equity intraday and positional strategies
  • F&O options writing and hedging algorithms
  • Index-based strategies (Nifty, Bank Nifty, Sensex)
  • Commodity trading algorithms on MCX
02

Quantitative Research & Backtesting

Statistical work on real Indian market history, with transaction costs applied from the first run rather than bolted on at the end to flatter a result.

Data AnalysisBacktestingMonte Carlo
  • Historical data analysis across 9+ years of NSE sessions
  • Walk-forward optimisation and out-of-sample validation
  • Monte Carlo simulation and drawdown distribution
  • Strategy performance attribution, cost and slippage modelling
03

Risk Management Frameworks

The controls that decide whether a good strategy survives a bad month. Built into the engine, fixed before deployment, and never loosened to rescue a losing position.

Stop-LossDrawdownMonitoring
  • Automated stop-loss and volatility-adjusted position sizing
  • Portfolio-level drawdown limits and kill switches
  • Real-time risk monitoring and end-of-day reconciliation
  • Circuit limit, liquidity and corporate-action safeguards
04

Consultation & Advisory

One-on-one sessions on strategy design, infrastructure and where an existing system is quietly leaking money to costs, slippage or overfitting.

Strategy ReviewEducationSEBI
  • Strategy review and independent performance audit
  • Market microstructure and execution education
  • Algo trading infrastructure and broker/API guidance
  • SEBI compliance orientation for automated trading
